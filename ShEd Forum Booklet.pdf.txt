The ShED Forum (Shershahbadi Educational Dialogue Forum)
is a community-led educational initiative established in 2018
that advances educational awareness and promotes higher
education among the Shershahbadi community, which faces
significant educational and socio-economic challenges and
remains underrepresented in higher education institutions.
The forum believes that education is the most powerful
instrument for overcoming marginalization, expanding
opportunities, and enabling sustainable social progress.
By fostering dialogue, mentorship, guidance, and educational
awareness, the Forum seeks to inspire students to pursue
higher education and realize their academic aspirations. We
believe that empowering individuals through education is the
most effective pathway to strengthening the community as a
whole and ensuring its meaningful participation in the
broader social, intellectual, and economic life of the nation.

OUR INITIATIVE
The ShED Forum undertakes a range of educational initiatives aimed at
expanding access to higher education and creating opportunities for
students from the Shershahbadi community.
At the core of our work is the SCG Center, which provides free
academic support, coaching, and guidance to students.
The Forum conducts ground campaigns to identify students at the
grassroots level and connect them with educational opportunities.
Through our Career Counseling and Admission Help Desk initiatives,
students receive assistance in choosing academic pathways,
understanding admission procedures, and navigating opportunities in
universities and colleges across the country. Our mentorship programs
provide continuous support to students through experienced mentors
who guide them in their academic and professional journeys. 
The Forum also promotes personal growth through its English Spoken
Program, which focuses on communication and soft-skill development.
In addition, the Alumni Connect initiative creates a strong network of
graduates and professionals who help students with subject selection,
higher studies, career planning, and personal development. 
Beyond academic initiatives, the forum organizes annual events such
as International Women’s Day Celebrations,and Foundation Day
Celebrations, which bring together students, alumni, and community
members to reflect on achievements and strengthen collective
commitment to educational empowerment.
The forum facilitates financial aid support through contributions from
alumni, patrons, founders, and well-wishers. 
Through these initiatives, ShED Forum continues to build a supportive
ecosystem that encourages learning, mentorship, leadership, and
educational advancement within the community.

IDEA BEHIND SHED FORUM
The Idea of ShED Forum (Shershahbadi Educational Dialogue Forum)
emerged when a few Shershahbadi students at Maulana Azad
National Urdu University (MANUU) realized that students from their
community were extremely limited in higher education. This
highlighted the distance between the Shershahbadi community and
universities.
Primarily residing in Seemanchal, Bihar, and parts of West Bengal
and Jharkhand, the community has long faced educational and
socio-economic challenges. Lack of awareness, guidance,
exposure, and opportunities often prevented talented and aspiring
students from accessing higher education.
The Forum was envisioned as a bridge connecting students with
information, guidance, mentorship, and opportunities. Its core
principle was that every student who secures university admission
should help at least one more student enter higher education the
following year, creating a culture of mentorship and collective
responsibility.
International Women’s Day was intentionally chosen as its
Foundation Day, reflecting the Forum’s commitment to improving
women’s participation in higher education, which remained
extremely limited.
Today, The Forum continues its founding vision of reducing the
distance between the community and universities. What began as a
small initiative has grown into an educational movement involving
students, alumni, mentors, and community members committed to
education, empowerment, opportunity, and social progress.

OUR PRESENCE
The ShED Forum has established its presence across the Seemanchal
region of Bihar, as well as in parts of West Bengal and Jharkhand,
where the Shershahbadi community resides. In addition to its
grassroots presence, the Forum has built student units in various
universities to strengthen student support, guidance, and
networking.
Through these university units, ShED Forum provides students with
guidance, mentorship, and support related to higher education and
academic opportunities.
University Units
UNIVERSITY UNITS
MANUU Unit (Maulana Azad National Urdu University, Hyderabad)
 Established in 2018, the MANUU Unit was the first university unit of
ShED Forum and currently has a student strength of approximately 200
students.
Jamia Unit (Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi)
 Established in 2021, the Jamia Unit currently has a student strength of
approximately 170 students.
HCU Unit (University of Hyderabad, Hyderabad)
 Established in 2019, the HCU Unit currently has a student strength of
approximately 15 students.
AMU Unit (Aligarh Muslim University, Aligarh)
 Established in 2022, the AMU Unit currently has a student strength of
approximately 220 students.
JNU Unit (Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi)
 Established in 2022, the JNU Unit currently has a student strength of
approximately 25 students.

DU Unit (University of Delhi, New Delhi)

Through these university units, ShED Forum continues to connect
students and provide guidance, mentorship, and support for higher

More than 1,000 students from the Shershahbadi community, who
remained connected with ShED Forum during their academic journey
across these universities, have successfully graduated. Today, through
the ShED Alumni Connect Program, they continue to contribute to and
strengthen this mission by guiding, mentoring, and supporting future
generations of students.
